## Trust order (highest wins)
> Confirm or adapt the default below. Applied mechanically: higher tier wins; a **superseded** source
> loses regardless of tier; a governing source's **silence** outranks a low-tier page that merely
> mentions the topic. (Full rules: `docs/method.md`.)

`law > regulation > policy > standard > procedure > approved pattern > wiki/how-to >
individual guidance > legacy/mirror`
